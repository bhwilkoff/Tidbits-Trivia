Tidbits Support

Tidbits turns the whole of Wikipedia into a trivia game. Here's help.

Get in touch

Email ben@learningischange.com — questions, bug reports, or a question that looks wrong (include the topic so we can fix it).

Common questions

Do I need an account or internet?

No. Tidbits works fully offline and has no account required. Signing in is optional and only needed for cross-device sync, leaderboards, or a Tidbits Club membership. The only feature that always needs the internet is "Create a quiz" from a topic.

Where do the questions come from?

They're generated from Wikipedia and Wikidata — real, sourced facts. Every question ends with the fact and a link to read more.

A question looks wrong. What do I do?

Email us the topic and what looked off. Because questions are built from live encyclopedic data, occasional edge cases slip through and we fix them.

Is my streak saved if I switch devices?

If you sign in with the same account (Sign in with Apple or Google), yes — your streak, stats, and Tidbits Club membership follow you across devices. Without signing in, progress stays on that one device.

Privacy

The free game collects nothing. Signing in, leaderboards, and Tidbits Club involve a small amount of account data — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what and why.

Delete your account or data

Tidbits doesn't require an account, so uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device. If you signed in and want your synced account data (profile, stats, Club entitlement) deleted from our servers too, email ben@learningischange.com from the address you signed in with (or tell us your display name) and we'll delete it. We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days.
